Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

The short version

WOD Clock is a fully offline app. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following data locally on your device only:

  • Timer configurations and named presets you create.
  • Your workout log: results, round splits, and notes you write.
  • App settings (language, sound preferences).

This data never leaves your device through the app. Deleting the app deletes all of it. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud / Google), which are governed by Apple's and Google's own policies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device to announce workout events (round starts, rest, finish) while the app is in the background. These notifications are generated on‑device and involve no server.

Microphone, camera, location

The app does not request or use the microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an online feature), the updated policy will be published on this page with a new date before the change takes effect.
